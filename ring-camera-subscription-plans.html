Ring Protect Features Explained

Video History & Cloud Storage (180 Days)

Both paid plans give you six months of recordings - 180 days of every motion event, doorbell press, and midnight cat sighting. Here's how cloud storage works:

  • Videos stored securely on Ring's encrypted servers
  • Access recordings from any device with the Ring app
  • Videos automatically delete after 180 days
  • Download important clips to save permanently before they expire
  • Share clips directly via link, text, or social media

Person Detection

This might be the feature worth paying for alone. Without Person Detection, your camera alerts you to all motion - tree branches, shadows, cars, leaves, the neighbor's cat at 3 AM. With Person Detection, Ring's AI filters the noise:

  • Identifies when motion is caused by a person vs. other movement
  • Reduces false alerts by up to 80% in our testing
  • Sends specific "Person Detected" notifications
  • Works on all Ring cameras with motion detection

Rich Notifications (Video Preview)

Instead of just a text alert, you see a video thumbnail directly in your notification. Glance at your phone and instantly know if it's the delivery driver or just wind blowing leaves - without opening the app.

Snapshot Capture

Between motion events, your camera snaps periodic photos throughout the day. Package delivered but no motion triggered? The snapshot probably caught it. Curious what time the landscapers showed up? Scroll through the visual timeline. It fills the gaps that motion-only recording misses.

Extended Warranty (Plus & Pro Only)

Ring devices normally come with a one-year warranty. With Plus or Pro, that warranty effectively never expires as long as you're subscribed:

  • Covers device malfunctions beyond the standard 1-year warranty
  • Free replacement if devices fail
  • Continues as long as your subscription is active
  • Covers all Ring devices linked to your account

10% Product Discount (Plus & Pro Only)

Planning to expand your Ring ecosystem? Plus and Pro subscribers get 10% off all purchases at Ring.com. Over time, this discount can offset a significant portion of your subscription cost.

Professional Monitoring (Pro Only)

This is where Pro justifies its $200/year price tag - but only if you want traditional security company features without traditional security company pricing:

  • 24/7 monitoring center watches your alarm system
  • Automatic police, fire, or medical dispatch when alarms trigger

  • Works even if your Wi-Fi or power goes out (cellular backup)

Is Ring Protect Worth It? Our Honest Verdict

The Verdict: Is Ring Camera Subscription Worth It?

8.5/10

Yes, Ring Protect is worth it for most users. The combination of video history, Person Detection, and 180-day storage provides genuine value that justifies the cost. However, the "right" plan depends entirely on your setup.

Ring Protect IS Worth It If:

  • You need to review footage: Missed a delivery? Need to see what triggered an alert at 2 AM? Without video history, that footage doesn't exist. Period.
  • Security is your actual priority: If something bad happens, recorded video is the difference between "I think someone was there" and handing police actual evidence.
  • You have 2+ cameras: At $100/year for unlimited devices, Plus is borderline ridiculous value - less than $8.50/month for your entire property.
  • Motion alerts are driving you insane: Person Detection transforms an annoying notification machine into something useful.
  • You want warranty protection: The extended warranty alone could save you $100+ on a single device replacement.

You Might Skip Ring Protect If:

  • Budget is genuinely tight: Live view is still real security. You can still see who's there - you just can't replay it later.
  • You only care about real-time monitoring: If your goal is "alert me when someone's at the door so I can check," the free tier does exactly that.
  • You're testing Ring before committing: Use the free features (or 30-day trial) first. See if you actually miss recordings.

Our Recommendation: For most Ring camera owners, Ring Protect Plus at $100/year is the sweet spot. Unlimited devices, extended warranty, 10% discount on future purchases, and assisted monitoring if you have Ring Alarm. Single camera? Basic at $49.99/year is perfectly adequate.

How to Sign Up for Ring Protect

Getting started with Ring Protect takes about 2 minutes. Here's how:

Open the Ring App

Launch the Ring app on your iOS or Android device. Make sure you're logged into your Ring account.

Go to Account Settings

Tap the three-line menu icon (hamburger menu), then tap "Account" or your profile icon.

Select "Subscriptions"

Under your account settings, find and tap "Subscriptions" or "Ring Protect Plans."

Choose Your Plan

Select Basic, Plus, or Pro. For Basic, you'll also need to choose which specific device to cover.

Select Billing Cycle

Choose monthly or annual billing. Annual saves you money - we recommend it if you're committed.

Enter Payment Information

Add your credit card or link your Amazon account for payment. Your plan activates immediately.

New Device Owners: Most new Ring cameras and doorbells include a 30-day free trial of Ring Protect. The trial starts automatically when you set up your device - use it to experience the full feature set before paying.

How to Cancel Ring Subscription

Canceling is straightforward with no hoops to jump through. Here's the process:

Open Ring App and Go to Account

Tap the menu, then "Account" to access your settings.

Tap "Subscriptions"

Find your active Ring Protect plan in the list.

Select "Cancel Plan"

Tap on your active subscription, then find and tap the cancel option.

Confirm Cancellation

Review what you'll lose access to, then confirm. Monthly plans end immediately; annual plans provide a prorated refund.

Before You Cancel: Download any video clips you want to keep. After cancellation, you have a short grace period to save recordings, then your cloud storage is permanently deleted. Don't lose important footage!

Ring Cancellation Policy

  • Prorated refunds: Annual subscribers get refunded for unused months

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Ring cameras work without a subscription?

Yes. Your Ring camera provides live view, motion alerts, two-way audio, and motion zones without any subscription. The limitation is no video recording - you can watch live but can't review footage later.

How much is Ring camera subscription per month?

Ring Protect Basic is $4.99/month for one device. Ring Protect Plus is $10/month for unlimited devices at one location. Ring Protect Pro is $20/month and includes professional monitoring.

What happens to my videos if I cancel Ring Protect?

You get a grace period to download any videos you want to keep. After that window closes, all cloud recordings are permanently deleted. New recordings won't be saved after cancellation.

Can I use Ring Protect at multiple addresses?

Ring Protect Plus covers all devices at one physical address. If you have a vacation home or second property, you'll need a separate subscription for that location. Each "location" in the Ring app is treated independently.

Is there a Ring Protect free trial?

Yes - most new Ring devices include a 30-day free trial of Ring Protect. This activates automatically when you set up your new camera or doorbell. Use this trial to experience video recording and Person Detection before committing.

Can I switch between Ring Protect plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade anytime. When upgrading, the new plan starts immediately. When downgrading, you'll receive a prorated credit for the difference.

Does Ring Protect work with Ring Alarm?

Yes. Ring Protect Plus includes assisted monitoring for Ring Alarm (the monitoring center calls you when alarm triggers). Ring Protect Pro adds professional monitoring with automatic police/fire dispatch.

Is Ring Protect required for Ring Alarm?

No, Ring Alarm works without a subscription for self-monitoring. However, you won't get cellular backup or professional monitoring without Ring Protect Plus or Pro.

How long does Ring store video?

Ring Protect stores video for 180 days (6 months). After 180 days, recordings automatically delete. Download important clips before they expire if you need to keep them permanently.

Can I share my Ring Protect subscription with family?

Ring Protect covers all devices on your Ring account. You can add Shared Users who can access your cameras and view recordings without needing their own subscription.
