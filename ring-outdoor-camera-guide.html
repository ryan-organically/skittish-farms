Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Ring outdoor camera batteries last?

Battery life varies based on activity and settings, but most users report 3-6 months between charges. High-traffic areas with frequent motion events will drain batteries faster.

Can Ring outdoor cameras work without Wi-Fi?

No, Ring cameras require a Wi-Fi connection to function. However, some advanced models can use Ring's Chime Pro as a Wi-Fi extender if your outdoor camera is far from your router.

Are Ring outdoor cameras waterproof?

Ring outdoor cameras are water-resistant (IP65 rated), meaning they can handle rain, snow, and humidity. However, they should not be submerged in water.

Do Ring cameras work in complete darkness?

Yes, all Ring outdoor cameras feature infrared night vision. Spotlight and Floodlight models also provide color night vision when their lights are activated.
