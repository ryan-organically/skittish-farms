When Totaltech Makes Financial Sense

Let's do the math. Standard Ring doorbell installation runs $99.99 at Best Buy without Totaltech. A Floodlight Cam installation can hit $149.99 or more depending on electrical requirements. If you're installing:

Honest Assessment: If you're comfortable with basic DIY and only buying a single Ring product, skip Totaltech. But if the thought of drilling into stucco or figuring out transformer voltages makes you queasy, the peace of mind is worth every penny.

Finding Open-Box Ring Products

Open-Box inventory varies wildly by location and timing. Here's how to maximize your chances:

  • Check online first: BestBuy.com shows Open-Box availability by store. Search for your Ring product, then filter by "Open-Box."
  • Visit after holidays: January and the weeks after Prime Day see massive return volumes. That's when Open-Box shelves overflow.
  • Ask in-store: Not all Open-Box inventory makes it to the floor display. Ask an associate to check the back.
  • Inspect before buying: You can open the box in-store. Check for all accessories, examine for damage, and test power if possible.

Open-Box Strategy: For security cameras that will be mounted 15 feet up on your house, "Satisfactory" grade is perfectly fine. No one will ever see the tiny scuff on the bottom. Save that 25% for something that matters.
