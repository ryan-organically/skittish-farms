Local vs Cloud Storage: You Own Your Footage

This is the philosophical core of the Reolink value proposition. With Ring and Nest, your security footage lives on their servers. You're essentially renting access to your own data. Miss a payment? Footage is gone. Company gets hacked? Your data is exposed. Want to keep footage longer than 60 days? Pay more.

Reolink flips this model:

Local Storage Options

  • MicroSD cards: Up to 256GB in most cameras. At 4K, that's 2-7 days of continuous recording.
  • NVR systems: Up to 12TB drives supporting weeks or months of footage
  • NAS integration: Works with Synology, QNAP, and other NAS systems via RTSP
  • FTP upload: Automatically push footage to your own server

Optional Cloud (If You Want It)

Reolink does offer cloud storage for those who want it - but it's optional, cheaper than competitors, and you're never locked in.

  • Basic: $3.49/month for 7 days of cloud storage
  • Standard: $6.99/month for 30 days
  • Premier: $10.49/month for 60 days

The Smart Play: Use local storage as your primary (it's free and always available), then add cheap cloud as emergency backup. If someone steals your camera and the SD card with it, the cloud copy is your evidence. But you're never forced into the subscription to use basic features.
