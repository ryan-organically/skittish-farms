Installation Guide

Nest Cam (Battery) Outdoor Installation

Good news: this is genuinely easy. We're talking "finished before your coffee gets cold" easy.

  1. Download the Google Home app (you probably already have it)
  2. Scan the QR code on the camera—it's right there, you can't miss it
  3. Connect to Wi-Fi and wait for the camera to do its thing
  4. Find your spot (7-10 feet high is the sweet spot for catching faces)
  5. Mount the magnetic plate with two screws—that's it, two
  6. Snap the camera on and angle it where you want. Done.

Nest Cam with Floodlight Installation

Okay, this one's different. Unless you're comfortable with electrical work, call a pro. Seriously. Here's what's involved:

  1. Turn off power at circuit breaker
  2. Remove existing light fixture
  3. Connect wires (hot, neutral, ground)
  4. Mount the floodlight bracket
  5. Attach the camera and floodlight assembly
  6. Restore power and complete app setup

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Nest cameras outside without Nest Aware?

Absolutely. This is one of Nest's underrated wins—you get 3 hours of free event video history without paying a dime. Real-time alerts, all the AI smarts, two-way audio. It all works. The subscription just extends how far back you can look and adds goodies like familiar face detection.

Are Nest outdoor cameras waterproof?

Weather-resistant, yes. Submarine, no. The IP54 rating means rain and dust won't kill it, but don't mount it where your sprinklers hit it directly or where water pools. Under an eave is ideal. Think "I can leave it outside" not "I can throw it in a pool."

How does Nest compare to Ring for outdoor use?

Honest answer? Both are excellent. Nest wins on brains (on-device AI, free storage), Ring wins on variety and budget-friendly options. The real question is simpler: do you say "Hey Google" or "Alexa" when you walk in the door? Start there.
