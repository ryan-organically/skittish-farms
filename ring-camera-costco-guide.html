What Ring Products Does Costco Carry?

Walk into your local Costco warehouse and you'll find Ring products, but probably not the full lineup you see on Amazon. Costco is selective about what they stock, focusing on bundles and multi-packs that offer clear value over buying individual items. Here's what you'll typically find:

Ring Video Doorbells

The doorbell category is where Costco really shines. They consistently stock:

  • Ring Video Doorbell (2nd Gen) - Often bundled with a Ring Chime
  • Ring Video Doorbell 4 - Usually the standard version, sometimes with extras
  • Ring Video Doorbell Pro 2 - The premium wired option, occasionally available
  • Ring Battery Doorbell Plus - Their newer head-to-toe video model

Ring Security Cameras

Camera availability varies more than doorbells, but here's what rotates through Costco shelves:

  • Ring Stick Up Cam - Battery, plug-in, or solar versions in bundles
  • Ring Spotlight Cam - Often in 2-packs or bundled with accessories
  • Ring Floodlight Cam - Less common in-store, more available online
  • Ring Indoor Cam - Usually sold in 2-packs

Ring Alarm Systems

Costco regularly carries Ring Alarm kits, typically with more pieces than the standard Amazon configurations:

  • Ring Alarm 8-piece or 14-piece kits - Base station plus sensors
  • Ring Alarm Pro - With built-in eero Wi-Fi router

Inventory Tip: Costco's Ring selection changes seasonally. The best variety appears in October through December (holiday shopping) and April through June (home improvement season). Check Costco.com for current availability before making the trip.

Costco Ring Camera Bundles Explained

Here's the thing about Costco: they rarely sell standalone Ring products. Instead, they negotiate exclusive bundles that aren't available anywhere else. These bundles typically include extras that would cost you money elsewhere. Let's break down the most common ones:

Best Value: Ring Video Doorbell + Chime Bundle

Costco's most popular Ring bundle. Includes the doorbell, Ring Chime (normally $30), and often an extra faceplate.

Typical Costco Ring Bundle Configurations

  • Doorbell + Chime: The entry-level bundle. Chime plugs into any outlet and alerts you when someone rings, even if your phone is silent.
  • Doorbell + Stick Up Cam: Great for covering front door plus one additional area. Costco sometimes adds a solar panel to sweeten the deal.
  • 2-Pack Camera Bundles: Two Spotlight Cams or Stick Up Cams at a discount. Perfect for covering multiple areas at once.
  • Alarm Kit + Camera: The home security starter pack. Alarm system plus one camera to watch the main entry point.

Watch Out: Costco bundles sometimes include older generation products. Always check the model number. A "Ring Video Doorbell" without a number is the basic 2nd gen model, not the newer Doorbell 4 or Plus models.

Costco Membership Benefits for Ring Cameras

Beyond the bundle pricing, your Costco membership comes with perks that can make Ring purchases significantly more attractive than other retailers. Here's what you're actually getting:

The Return Policy Advantage

This is the big one. Costco's electronics return policy is generous compared to Amazon's 30-day window or Best Buy's 15 days:

  • 90-day return window for electronics, including Ring cameras
  • No questions asked - Changed your mind? Don't like the video quality? They'll take it back
  • Full refund including any bundle accessories
  • No restocking fee unlike some other retailers

Pro Tip: If you're unsure about Ring vs. other brands, buy from Costco. Test it for 2-3 months during different seasons. If it doesn't meet your needs, return it and try something else. You can't do that anywhere else.

Costco Concierge Services

Buy a Ring camera at Costco and you get access to Costco Concierge Services for technical support. It's not Ring's support (you still have that too), but Costco's team can help with:

  • Basic setup and troubleshooting
  • Coordinating returns if Ring support can't solve the problem
  • Extended warranty claims through Costco's system

Executive Member 2% Reward

Executive members earn 2% back on Costco purchases, including electronics. On a $200 Ring bundle, that's $4 back. Not life-changing, but it adds up if you're buying multiple cameras or a full alarm system.

Costco Citi Card Benefits

Pay with the Costco Anywhere Visa and you get:

  • 2% cash back on Costco purchases (4% total with Executive membership)
  • Extended warranty - Adds 2 years to manufacturer warranty on electronics
  • Purchase protection - Covers damage or theft for 120 days

Extended Warranty Math

Ring cameras come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. The Costco Citi card adds 2 more years automatically. That's 3 years of coverage on a $100-200 camera, for free. Amazon and Best Buy can't match this without selling you an extended warranty.

Online vs In-Warehouse Availability

Costco's online and warehouse inventories are surprisingly different. Understanding this can save you a wasted trip or help you score a better deal.

Costco.com Advantages

  • Larger selection: Online-only bundles and configurations not found in warehouses
  • Stock visibility: See exactly what's available before driving anywhere
  • Exclusive online deals: Some promotions are web-only
  • Ship to home: Free shipping on most Ring products over $75

In-Warehouse Advantages

  • See before you buy: Check box contents and included accessories
  • Instant gratification: Walk out with it today
  • Warehouse-only deals: Occasional in-store markdowns not reflected online
  • No shipping damage risk: Inspect the box before purchase

Best Strategy: Check Costco.com first to see what's available and at what price. If the item is in stock locally and prices match, buy in-store to avoid shipping. If it's an online-exclusive bundle, order for delivery.

Checking Local Inventory

Costco's website shows real-time warehouse inventory. Here's how to check:

  1. Go to Costco.com and search for Ring
  2. Click on the product you want
  3. Look for "Check Warehouse Availability" link
  4. Enter your zip code to see nearby stock

Note: This feature shows if it's in stock, but not how many units remain. Popular items during sales can sell out quickly.

Should You Buy Ring at Costco?

After analyzing hundreds of price points and member benefits, here's our straightforward advice:

Buy Ring at Costco If:

  • You're already a Costco member (don't join just for Ring)
  • You want a bundle with multiple items or accessories
  • The 90-day return window matters to you
  • You have the Costco Citi card (extended warranty is huge)
  • You're buying during Black Friday or a documented sale
  • You want a Ring Alarm system (Costco's prices are consistently best)

Buy Ring on Amazon If:

  • You're not a Costco member
  • You want the newest model immediately
  • You're buying a single camera at regular price
  • Prime Day or a flash sale is happening
  • You need a specific model Costco doesn't carry
