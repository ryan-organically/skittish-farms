Ring Camera Black Friday Strategy

Black Friday Ring deals deserve special attention because they're predictable and substantial. Here's what we've learned from tracking Black Friday camera deals over the past several years:

  • Deals start early: Amazon typically begins discounting Ring products 1-2 weeks before Thanksgiving. Don't wait for Black Friday itself.
  • Doorbell deals are best: The Ring Video Doorbell consistently sees the deepest discounts (often 50% off).
  • Bundle pricing peaks: Multi-camera bundles and doorbell + camera combos hit their lowest prices of the year.
  • Stock runs out: Popular items like the Ring Indoor Cam and Stick Up Cam frequently sell out. Buy early in the sale window.
  • Price-match opportunities: Best Buy and Target often match Amazon's Ring pricing during Black Friday week.

Timing Warning: If you're reading this outside a major sale event and don't need a camera urgently, waiting for the next Prime Day or Black Friday can save you $30-80 per camera. Set a calendar reminder.

Should You Buy Renewed?

Here's our honest take after testing both new and renewed Ring cameras:

  • Functionally identical: We couldn't tell the difference in video quality, app performance, or reliability between new and renewed units.
  • Cosmetic imperfections: Some renewed cameras have minor scuffs or scratches. If it's going outside anyway, who cares?
  • Shorter warranty: 90 days vs. 1 year for new. If something's going to fail, it usually happens quickly, so this is less risky than it sounds.
  • Stacking with sales: Renewed cameras go on sale too. Renewed + sale pricing = maximum savings.

Best Use Case: Renewed cameras are perfect for secondary coverage areas (garages, sheds, guest houses) where you want security but don't need the latest features.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to buy a Ring camera?

Prime Day (July) and Black Friday (November) offer the deepest discounts, typically 40-50% off. If you can't wait, the October Prime Big Deal Days event is nearly as good.

Are Ring cameras cheaper on Black Friday or Prime Day?

Prices are usually identical. The main difference is inventory. Black Friday has more stock but more competition. Prime Day is Prime member exclusive, so less competition for deals.

Should I buy a refurbished Ring camera?

Yes, if saving money matters more than having a factory-sealed box. Amazon Renewed Ring cameras work identically to new ones and save 20-40%.

Do Ring cameras ever go on sale at full price?

Not really. Outside of major sale events, the best you'll typically see is 15-20% off individual models. Significant discounts (30%+) only happen during scheduled Amazon shopping events.

Is it better to buy cameras individually or in bundles?

Bundles almost always offer better per-camera pricing. If you need 2+ cameras, look for bundles first.
