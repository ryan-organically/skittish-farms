Placement Tip: Mount PIR sensors 6-7 feet high, perpendicular to the path people walk. PIR detects motion across its field of view better than motion directly toward it. Don't point at windows—sunlight causes false triggers.

What to Avoid

Not everything on Amazon Haul is worth buying. Here's what we tested and rejected:

Avoid These:

  • Ultra-cheap PIR sensors under $5: Detection is inconsistent—they either miss motion or trigger constantly
  • Sensors with "WiFi app control": Apps are usually abandoned, servers shut down within months
  • Microwave motion sensors at this price: Require proper calibration that budget units skip
  • Sensors claiming "pet immune" under $10: They're not—your cat will trigger false alarms

Why Budget Sensors Fail

The main issues with sub-$10 sensors:

  • Poor PIR lens quality: Detection zones have gaps or excessive sensitivity
  • Cheap electronics: Temperature changes cause false triggers
  • Bad battery contacts: Batteries lose connection randomly
  • Weak wireless range: Claimed ranges are measured in open air, not through walls

The sensors we recommend above have enough reviews to confirm they work reliably. That's the advantage of high-volume Amazon Haul items—problems get exposed quickly.

How to Get the Most from Budget Sensors

Installation Tips:

  • Clean surfaces with alcohol before applying adhesive
  • Test the sensor BEFORE permanent mounting
  • Keep PIR sensors away from heat sources and direct sunlight
  • Buy quality batteries (Duracell/Energizer)—cheap batteries die fast
  • Replace batteries proactively every 6 months
