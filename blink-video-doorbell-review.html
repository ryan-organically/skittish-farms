Blink Video Doorbell Overview

Video Quality Analysis

At 1080p Full HD with a 135-degree field of view, the Blink Video Doorbell captures everything you need to see. Faces are identifiable, package labels are readable, and the wide angle means you won't miss visitors standing off to the side. Is it the sharpest video we've ever seen from a doorbell? No. Is it plenty sharp for security purposes? Absolutely.

Daytime Performance: Colors look natural, details stay crisp, and the camera handles harsh sunlight better than we expected. The HDR processing isn't as sophisticated as Ring's, but you'll still see faces clearly even with the sun behind them.

Nighttime Performance: Infrared night vision kicks in automatically when light drops. The black-and-white footage is surprisingly detailed up to about 15 feet. Beyond that, you're looking at shapes and movement rather than fine details. For most porch situations, it's more than adequate.

Two-Way Audio

Talk to visitors through your phone, wherever you are. The speaker quality is decent for a $50 device, though we'd describe it as "functional" rather than "impressive." Visitors can hear you clearly, and you can hear them. There's a slight delay, but nothing that makes conversations awkward. Pro tip: the half-duplex audio means you can't talk and listen simultaneously, so wait for the other person to finish before responding.

Motion Detection

The Blink Video Doorbell uses passive infrared (PIR) motion detection, which means it responds to body heat rather than pixel changes. In practice, this reduces false alerts from passing cars, swaying branches, and shifting shadows. You can adjust sensitivity levels and set activity zones to focus on areas that matter.

  • Motion zones let you define specific areas to monitor and ignore everything else
  • Sensitivity settings range from low to high, letting you find the sweet spot for your situation
  • Retrigger time controls how long the camera waits before recording another event
  • Night infrared intensity is adjustable if the IR LEDs create too much glare

Motion Tip: Start with medium sensitivity and adjust from there. Too high and you'll get alerts for every squirrel. Too low and you might miss the delivery driver. Most users find their sweet spot within a week of tweaking.

Alexa Integration

This is where Blink shines. Say "Alexa, show me the front door" to any Echo Show, and boom, live video. Someone presses the doorbell, and every Echo device in your house announces it. You can even answer the door through your Echo Show without touching your phone. If you're already in the Amazon ecosystem, the Blink Video Doorbell feels like it was designed specifically for you.

Installation Guide

Blink designed this doorbell for people who've never installed anything more complicated than a smoke detector. Whether you go battery or wired, you're looking at a 15-20 minute project tops. Here's how both options break down.

What's in the Box

  • Blink Video Doorbell
  • Two AA lithium batteries (pre-installed)
  • Mounting bracket and wedge
  • Mounting screws and anchors
  • Wiring connector for hardwired installation
  • Quick start guide

Battery Installation (Easiest Option)

  1. Download the Blink app and create an account (2 minutes)
  2. Add the doorbell to your account by scanning the QR code
  3. Connect to your Wi-Fi network
  4. Choose your mounting location (4-5 feet high is ideal)
  5. Use the included template to mark holes, then drill
  6. Attach the mounting bracket with the included screws
  7. Slide the doorbell onto the bracket until it clicks
  8. Adjust the angle using the wedge if needed

Battery Installation Tip: The included batteries are already inserted. Just remove the plastic pull tab to activate them. No need to buy anything extra right out of the box.

Wired Installation

  1. Turn off power to your existing doorbell at the circuit breaker
  2. Remove your old doorbell and note the two wires
  3. Connect the wires to the included wiring connector (polarity doesn't matter)
  4. Attach the mounting bracket to the wall
  5. Plug the wiring connector into the back of the Blink doorbell
  6. Slide the doorbell onto the bracket
  7. Turn the power back on at the circuit breaker
  8. Complete setup in the Blink app

Wired Installation Tip: The Blink Video Doorbell works with most mechanical and electronic chimes. If your existing chime doesn't work after installation, you might need the Blink Doorbell Chime Kit (sold separately) to boost the signal.
